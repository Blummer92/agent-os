The planner preserves owner-specified order after deterministic deduplication, requires exact canonical evidence for every requested PR, distinguishes candidate, already-terminal, item-local-blocked, and shared-blocked dispositions, and structurally requires pre-merge reacquisition for every planned item while hard-coding `merge_authorized=False` and `side_effects_performed=False`. Registered the module in the existing planning architecture domain and added focused regression coverage in `tests/agent_os_issue_acceptance/test_pr_batch_merge_plan.py`.
