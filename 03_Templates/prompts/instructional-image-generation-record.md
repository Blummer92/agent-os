# Instructional Image Generation Record

Use this template to prepare readable, reusable image-generation candidates for
instructional visual workflows and image-library handoffs.

Source and approval behavior remains governed by
`01_Shared_Standards/instructional-design/instructional-materials-sources.md`.
This template does not establish asset approval, rights, provenance, privacy, or
classroom readiness.

## Global Image Rules

Write constraints shared by the whole batch once. Keep them short and concrete.
Examples: photorealistic; believable everyday environments; appropriate for the
intended learners; no text, logos, watermarks, collage, or private identifying
information.

Do not repeat these global rules inside every image prompt unless a specific
record needs an exception or extra emphasis.

## Filled Example

### 01 - Leading Lines

**Asset ID:** `PHOTO-COMP-LINES-001`

**IMAGE PROMPT**

> Create a realistic photograph in an everyday school or public environment
> where strong architectural lines, railings, or a pathway guide the viewer
> toward one clearly visible subject. Use natural daylight and a believable
> student-photography perspective. Make the directional eye movement obvious
> enough for a beginning photography student to recognize leading lines, while
> keeping the scene natural rather than staged or diagram-like.

**AVOID**

- Surreal or impossible architecture.
- Lines that do not clearly lead toward the subject.

**ADD LATER**

- Subtle line traces or arrows only if the unannotated photograph needs extra
  instructional cueing.

**LIBRARY HANDOFF**

- Unit / lesson: Photography Foundations / Elements & Composition
- Asset role: Instructional photographic example
- Intended reuse: Challenge cards, slides, modeling, critique
- Candidate status: Needs governed review before classroom-ready use
- Review notes: Preserve a clean master; overlays belong on derivatives.

## Review Check

Before handoff, confirm that the record is readable at a glance, the prompt has
enough detail to control the essential visual idea, repeated global constraints
have not bloated the prompt, and any overlays are separated from the clean
master-image request.
