# Teacher Talk Rehearsal Template

## Purpose
Use this template when the teacher needs exact classroom-ready language, think-alouds, prompts, transitions, or rehearsal support for a live teaching moment.

## Use When
- Teacher language is stiff, overloaded, or hard to say aloud.
- The teacher needs a launch, transition, direction set, think-aloud, prompt, or close.
- The hardest live-teaching moment needs rehearsal.
- Likely confusion needs a stronger response line and teacher move.

## Do Not Use When
- The main request is a section-by-section audit.
- The first problem is choosing which file to use.
- The model sequence itself is missing or misaligned.
- The user wants whole-lesson redesign rather than spoken teacher language.

## Workflow
1. Identify the lesson goal and immediate student task.
2. Identify the exact live-teaching moment.
3. Ground the talk in objective, likely confusion, and next step.
4. Choose replacement language, play-by-play, or rehearsal coaching.
5. Write in natural spoken classroom language and make thinking visible.
6. Pair trouble spots with one response line, one teacher move, and one observable check.
7. Lead with usable wording before explanation.
8. End with the bounded Materials extract and active blockers when a handoff is needed.

## Default Rehearsal View
1. Lesson goal.
2. Immediate student task.
3. Play-by-play model.
4. Highest-impact improvements.
5. Primary misconception and response when required.
6. Bounded Materials extract.
7. Active blockers.

| Moment | Teacher says | Teacher does | Students do or notice | Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|

`Check` records observable evidence of student understanding or successful performance.

## Bounded Materials Extract
Include only selected approved teacher language, sentence frames, worked-example steps, misconception/correction examples, visual-anchor specifications, confirmed material-safe vocabulary references, blockers, and next owner. Use references plus only the excerpts needed for the next task. Preserve `execution_authorized: false`. Do not embed a full unit history, vocabulary map, or source document, and do not imply production, assessment, readiness, source-of-truth, or write authorization.

## Required And Optional Artifacts
A required missing artifact sets `BLOCKED`, names the exact artifact, and routes to Teacher Modeling Coach. Optional enhancements may be omitted or returned as warnings. Instructional Materials must not silently reconstruct required modeling.

## Quality Bar
The output should sound speakable, align tightly to what students do next, improve clarity without over-scripting, and give usable lines before coaching commentary. Rehearsal, audit, and materials-extract are views over one modeling record, not separate schemas. Numeric ranges are defaults, not validity limits.
